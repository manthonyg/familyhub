Family Hub

Our family's calendar, on the kitchen wall and in our pockets.

Family Hub is a small, private app for one household. It runs on our own Home Assistant and shows our shared Google family calendar on a kitchen tablet and our phones.

What it does

  • Shows the week at a glance, color-coded by person.
  • Lets us add events by typing one plain sentence.
  • Keeps everything in two-way sync with our Google family calendar.
  • Lets us tag events however we like.

Who it's for

Just our family. It isn't offered to the public and has no accounts to sign up for.
