Family Hub

Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

The short version

Family Hub only reads and writes the one Google calendar you choose, stores data on your own Home Assistant at home, and never sells or shares anything.

What we access

With your permission, Family Hub uses the Google Calendar API to read, create, edit and delete events on the single shared calendar you pick during setup. It does not access your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Where data lives

Calendar events and Family Hub settings (people, tags, colors) are stored on your own Home Assistant device in your home. The sign-in token Google issues is stored there too. Nothing is sent to servers run by Family Hub; there aren't any.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data only travels between your Home Assistant and Google to keep the calendar in sync.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the Family Hub integration from Home Assistant to erase its stored data.
